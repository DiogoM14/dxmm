title: What a team loses and gains when it leaves Scrum
date: 2026-06-02

---

Three cycles into Shape Up, some notes on what changed and what didn't.

The thing nobody warns you about is how quiet it gets. No daily standup, no sprint review, no retro every two weeks. For the first cycle that silence felt like something was broken. It wasn't. People were just working.

## Lost

Velocity as a number. Story points. The feeling of "done" every fortnight. A few people liked that rhythm and I understand why.

## Gained

Six weeks is long enough to finish something real and short enough that the appetite stays honest. Writing a pitch forces the shaping to happen before the work starts, which is where most of our Scrum sprints used to fall apart.

The cool-down is the part I'd defend hardest. Two weeks with no assigned work sounds like waste until you see what people fix when nobody is asking them to.

This whole blog is solely for me. I'm writing for myself, I don't want to write perfectly, I don't intend to be scientific about what I will say. If someone that's not me is reading this, keep in mind this was not written for you, even though you can see something interesting, that's purely a personal note.

Initially I started having a bunch of personal notes on my pc, at the very best, half of them were written by AI and I had a hard time remembering what was there. Because of that, this whole process of writing to this blog is intended to be slow, a slow process where I can really learn how to communicate better over time and thinking about what I'm writing.

Now, I want my personal notes public. I want to leave my comfort zone as well as having an easily accessible website with all my thoughts anywhere. At the best case scenario, I will have a dump of my brain here.

Hopefully, as the time goes by, the quality of each note and writing will improve. I can take this progress as a self awareness that I can improve every single aspect of life. With consistency.

I will write about things I like, so the topics may change over the time.

For this, I'm committing to use AI purely for grammatical errors, my main goal is to learn organizing my thoughts.
